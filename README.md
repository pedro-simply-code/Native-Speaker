# Native Speaker — homepage concepts

Twelve marketing homepage directions for a language-learning company that offers
live English and Spanish classes with native teachers, with Guatemala as its
home base.

All twelve are built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step, no
framework, no runtime dependencies, and no remote image hosts. Open any
`index.html` directly in a browser.

## Run it

Open the root `index.html` to see the twelve templates, then open any homepage:

- `lang-market/index.html`
- `lang-academy/index.html`
- `speak-spanish/index.html`
- `lang-learn/index.html`
- `make-talk/index.html`
- `speak-native/index.html`
- `talk-table/index.html`
- `lang-route/index.html`
- `bilingual-conversation/index.html`
- `lang-match/index.html`
- `tandem-natives/index.html`
- `lang-habit/index.html`

Or serve the folder locally:

```powershell
npx serve .
```

## The twelve concepts

The index groups them in four series: three readings of the existing market,
three product bets that go beyond a single lesson, three ways for people to meet
through Guatemala, and three low-friction ways to start practising.

### Series 01 — Three readings of the market

#### 01 — Lang Market (`lang-market/`)

Inspired by Preply and italki. A teacher marketplace rooted in Guatemala where
learners in the US and Guatemala compare profiles, filter by language, price,
and availability, and book a lesson.

- Conversation-first hero with a live-lesson composition and correction example.
- Teacher catalogue with English/Spanish filters, ratings, prices, and favourites,
  featuring teachers in Antigua, Livingston, Quetzaltenango, Lake Atitlán, and
  Guatemala City.
- Interactive bilingual lesson demo.
- Three-step onboarding, learner stories, pay-as-you-go pricing, and FAQ.
- Booking dialog with teacher-specific content, upcoming dates, and confirmation.
- Design: Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree, cool blue/mint palette, soft card surfaces.

#### 02 — Lang Academy (`lang-academy/`)

Inspired by Spanish Academy. A niche bilingual academy with age-based programs,
native Spanish and English teachers rooted in Guatemala, a structured
curriculum, one consistent teacher, progress reporting, and school partnerships.

- Warm editorial hero with an arched class photo, email capture, and progress card.
- Tabbed programs: Preschool, Elementary, Middle school, High school, Adults, Schools.
- Three-step enrollment, bento “method” section, teacher profiles, family reviews.
- Pricing with monthly/quarterly billing, 10% quarterly saving.
- Schools band, six-question FAQ, and booking dialog with program/plan context.
- Design: Fraunces + Karla, cream/terracotta/teal/marigold palette, arched imagery.

#### 03 — Speak Spanish (`speak-spanish/`)

A private-conversation product: one promise, 25 minutes with the same native
teacher from Guatemala, plus an AI practice partner between lessons. Spanish for
learners in the US, English for learners in Guatemala.

- Hero with a working 25-minute lesson timer and scenario switcher.
- Coach profiles with language filters and teacher preference handoff.
- Plan selection that carries into the level-test form.
- FAQ, AI practice proof, learner results, and a $1 first-month offer.
- Design: Instrument Serif + Archivo, signal red/lilac/butter/porcelain palette.

### Series 02 — Three product bets

#### 04 — Lang Learn (`lang-learn/`)

A six-week real-world immersion sprint in Guatemala. The language leaves the
classroom and becomes a set of missions in Antigua, Lake Atitlán, Quetzaltenango,
or online.

- Field-pass hero with route, level, and mission list.
- City tabs for Antigua, Lake Atitlán, and Quetzaltenango, each with its own missions.
- Week-by-week route and rhythm cards.
- Coach profiles, field notes, project kit, pricing, and six-question FAQ.
- City and plan selections carry into the enrollment form.
- Design: Newsreader + Space Grotesk, sand/forest/signal-orange palette.

#### 05 — Make Talk (`make-talk/`)

A small-group language studio built around making something each season.
Spanish from Guatemala and English for Guatemala, live online.

- Live-room hero with tonight’s prompt, seats, and season project.
- Four room tabs: Conversation Lab, Scene Study, Writing Room, Listening Room.
- Each room updates its schedule, mentor, project, and group shape.
- Project gallery, mentor profiles, weekly schedule, and Open Mic proof.
- Room and plan selections carry into the join form.
- Design: Bricolage Grotesque + DM Sans, ink/cream/lime/coral/lavender palette.

#### 06 — Speak Native (`speak-native/`)

A pronunciation and sound lab with native coaches. A ten-minute diagnosis, three
target sounds, and eight weeks of short repetitions, for Spanish and English
learners in the US and Guatemala.

- Waveform console hero with an interactive sound check and score.
- Sound map with four tabs: th, r, vowels, and rhythm.
- Working recording simulation that updates score and attempt count.
- Before/after proof, coach profiles, eight-week program, pricing, and FAQ.
- Plan selection carries into the booking form.
- Design: Sora + Lora, deep teal/cream/coral/mint palette.

### Series 03 — Three ways to meet through Guatemala

#### 07 — Talk Table (`talk-table/`)

Weekly conversation tables that pair learners in the US with native hosts in
Guatemala. Five or six people, one question, one hour. Spanish for US learners
and English for Guatemalan learners.

- Warm hero with a sample table, host, and weekly rhythm.
- Four table tabs: Café, Work, Culture, and Beginners, each with its own host,
  schedule, level, and prompts.
- Host profiles from Antigua, Quetzaltenango, and Lake Atitlán.
- Small-group pricing, a three-step join flow, and a six-question FAQ.
- Table and level selections carry into the join form.
- Design: DM Serif Display + Manrope, cream/green/apricot palette.

#### 08 — Lang Route (`lang-route/`)

A two-to-eight week Spanish immersion across Guatemala. One-to-one classes each
morning with a Guatemalan teacher, afternoons spent using the language in the
market, the comedor, and the bus stop.

- Route hero with a stamped field note and teacher quote.
- Three city tabs: Antigua, Lake Atitlán, and Quetzaltenango, each with its own
  day plan, stay, and route notes.
- Week-by-week rhythm, homestay and guesthouse options, and a classic route.
- Teacher profiles, route notes, pricing, and a six-question FAQ.
- City and duration selections carry into the join form.
- Design: Fraunces + Work Sans, paper/terracotta/teal/gold palette.

#### 09 — Bilingual Conversation (`bilingual-conversation/`)

Bilingual training for teams that work between the US and Guatemala. Spanish for
the US team, English for the Guatemala team, or both directions in one Meeting
lab.

- Bridge hero with a live session agenda and two-direction language toggle.
- Three programs: Work Spanish, Work English, and Meeting lab.
- Step-by-step rollout, session breakdown, manager reporting, and cohort pricing.
- Team-size and direction selections carry into the demo form.
- Design: Sora + Source Sans 3 + Source Serif 4, paper/navy/orange palette.

### Series 04 — Three ways to practise

#### 10 — Lang Match (`lang-match/`)

A guided teacher-matching concept. Three questions about goal, learning style,
and schedule produce a shortlist with a match score, instead of a catalogue to
browse.

- Interactive three-question quiz with a Spanish/English switch.
- Result card with teacher, location, quote, tags, and match score.
- Restart flow, sample shortlist, pricing, learner stories, and FAQ.
- Free rematch promise; English teachers are native speakers based in the US.
- Design: Urbanist + Public Sans, indigo/periwinkle/peach/mint palette.

#### 11 — Tandem Natives (`tandem-natives/`)

A guided two-way language exchange. Two people trade thirty minutes of English
for thirty minutes of Spanish, with a host who sets the prompts and keeps time.

- Exchange card with a live pair, order switch, and two-half timeline.
- Four formats: Casual, Work, Exam, and Culture, each with prompts and pricing.
- Host profiles from Guatemala and the US, plus safety and hosting details.
- Exchange stories, pack pricing, FAQ, and a partner-matching form.
- Design: Outfit + Mulish, teal/coral/butter/paper palette.

#### 12 — Lang Habit (`lang-habit/`)

A daily micro-practice habit with a weekly live class. Ten minutes on a phone
each day, one 45-minute group class on Sunday, and a teacher who remembers the
week.

- Today card with streak, week dots, phrase, listen state, and a done toggle.
- Seven day tabs, each with a theme, phrase, tasks, and practice focus.
- Spanish/English switch updates every phrase in the week.
- Live-class card, coach profiles, learner stories, pricing, and FAQ.
- Free daily plan; paid plans add the live class and a private hour.
- Design: Albert Sans + Instrument Serif, sky/lime/coral palette.

## Shared interactions

- Sticky header state and accessible mobile navigation with a 400 ms transition.
- Tabs use `aria-selected`, roving tabindex, and keyboard support where relevant.
- Accordion FAQ panels update `aria-expanded`, `aria-hidden`, and `inert`.
- Forms validate inline, focus the first invalid field, and show a success state.
- Plan and option selections carry context into the final form.
- Scroll reveal runs only when JavaScript is available and respects reduced motion.
- Responsive from 1440px down to 320px, with visible focus styles.
- WCAG 2 A/AA axe scans return no violations on all twelve pages and the index.
- No horizontal overflow at 320–1440px; rotated marquee elements are clipped.

## Adding another concept

Create a new folder with its own `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js`,
add a card to the root `index.html` grid, point all `assets/` references at the
shared root, and add a 1440×936 preview image under `assets/img/concepts/`.
Keep the root `index.html` as the entry point.

## Content note

Company names, teacher profiles, reviews, prices, phone numbers, and school
partnerships are demonstration content. Replace them with real data before
publishing. Photography is stored locally under `assets/`; review the original
licences before commercial use.
