The real cost

The meeting works.
The understanding doesn’t.

Most bilingual teams do not need another app. They need a shared habit: the confidence to speak up in the other language, and the patience to let a sentence take longer.

  • Half the room goes quiet

    The people with the least confidence have the most context. Meetings drift toward the loudest accent.

  • Every call costs double

    Repeating, translating, and rewriting decisions eats the hour you needed for the work.

  • Training is not the work

    Generic courses teach travel Spanish. Your team needs the vocabulary of a sprint review.
