How it works

Small enough
to actually do.

No forty-minute video, no twelve-step lesson. One prompt, one live hour, and a streak that does not punish you for missing a day.

01

Pick your ten minutes

Morning coffee, lunch break, or the ride home. Lang Habit sends one prompt at the time you choose.

2 minutes to set up
02

Say one thing out loud

A phrase, a question, a thirty-second voice note. You practise the sentence you will actually use.

10 minutes a day
03

Bring it to the live class

Sunday, 45 minutes, a small group and a native teacher. Everything you practised that week gets used.

Once a week

This week

Seven prompts.
One real conversation.

Each day has a theme, one phrase, and two things to do with it. Switch the language to see the same week in English.

Day 1 · 10 minutes · A1–C2

Introduce yourself in one breath.

Name, city, and one thing you are doing this week. That is enough to open almost any conversation.

Me llamo Ana y vivo en Austin.

My name is Ana and I live in Austin.

  • 01 Say it with your real name and city.
  • 02 Add what you are doing this week.
  • 03 Record a 20-second voice note.

Day 2 · 10 minutes · A1–C2

Order the coffee you actually want.

Milk, size, sugar, and one question. The vocabulary of the first ten minutes of every morning.

Un café con leche, por favor. ¿Tienen leche de avena?

A coffee with milk, please. Do you have oat milk?

  • 01 Order it out loud, slowly.
  • 02 Change the milk to oat, soy, or none.
  • 03 Ask a follow-up question.

Day 3 · 10 minutes · A1–C2

Ask for directions without apologising.

One polite opener, one place, and one follow-up so you understand the answer the first time.

Perdón, ¿cómo llego al mercado?

Excuse me, how do I get to the market?

  • 01 Ask for a place near your home.
  • 02 Repeat the answer back to confirm.
  • 03 Thank them and say how far it is.

Day 4 · 10 minutes · A2–C2

Give a status update in four lines.

What you did, what you are doing, what is blocking you, and what you need. Short enough for a real meeting.

Terminé la propuesta. Ahora reviso el presupuesto.

I finished the proposal. Now I am reviewing the budget.

  • 01 Use a real task from this week.
  • 02 Add one blocker and one ask.
  • 03 Say it again in thirty seconds.

Day 5 · 10 minutes · A1–C2

Make a plan with another person.

Suggest, agree, and set a time. The three moves that turn a nice chat into an actual plan.

¿Nos vemos el sábado a las diez?

Shall we meet on Saturday at ten?

  • 01 Suggest a day and a time.
  • 02 Move it once, politely.
  • 03 Confirm who brings what.

Day 6 · 10 minutes · A2–C2

Tell a story in the past tense.

Something small that happened this week. Beginning, middle, and how you felt at the end.

Ayer caminé hasta el lago y me quedé una hora.

Yesterday I walked to the lake and stayed for an hour.

  • 01 Pick one real thing from your week.
  • 02 Use three past-tense verbs.
  • 03 End with how it felt.
