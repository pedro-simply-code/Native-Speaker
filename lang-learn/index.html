The field method

You don’t need another lesson. You need a place to use one.

Fluency is not a certificate. It is the moment the barista asks a follow-up question and you answer without translating in your head.

A

Do first, study after

Every mission comes before the grammar. You meet the gap in real life, then your coach names it.

B

Small enough to repeat

Ten minutes at a café beats two hours of theory you never use. Repetition is built into the week.

C

A coach who was there

Your debrief is built from your own messages, receipts, and half-finished sentences — not a generic syllabus.
