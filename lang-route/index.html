A week, honestly

Mornings are for
the hard part.

Class happens early, while your head is clear. The rest of the day is for using it. Nothing here is a “cultural activity” you sit through politely — it is the lesson, moved outside.

  • Mon
    Arrive and speak badly

    A short walk, a long introduction, and the first correction you will remember.

  • Tue
    The market lesson

    Prices, quantities, and the polite way to ask for a better one.

  • Wed
    Story day

    Tell your teacher something that happened. Then tell it again, better.

  • Thu
    Go somewhere

    A village, a viewpoint, a workshop. The teacher comes; the grammar follows.

  • Fri
    Dinner with the table

    You cook one dish from home. They cook one from here. You talk about both.

“You do not learn a language by looking at it. You learn it by needing it at the market.”

Diego · teacher, Quetzaltenango
