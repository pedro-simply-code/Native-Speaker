The studio model

A class should
leave you with
something.

Not a page of notes. A scene you performed, a question you asked well, a paragraph someone else understood. The language is the material; the thing you make is the point.

01

Warm up out loud

Ten minutes of low-stakes talking. No introductions, no syllabus. A question everyone answers badly, then better.

02

Make the thing

Pairs or small groups work on the night’s task while the mentor moves between rooms, correcting in the moment.

03

Show it, then keep it

The last twenty minutes are for sharing. Every project goes into your studio shelf, with the mentor’s notes attached.
