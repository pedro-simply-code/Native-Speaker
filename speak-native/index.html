Sound 01 · dental fricative

Think, not sink.

The voiceless TH is a tongue-tip sound, not a /t/ and not an /s/. Spanish, French, Italian and many other first languages replace it with something nearby. The result is understandable, but it costs you a beat.

  • think /θɪŋk/ vs. sink /sɪŋk/
  • three /θriː/ vs. tree /triː/
  • mouth /maʊθ/ vs. mouse /maʊs/
Mouth note

Tongue tip lightly between the teeth. Blow air, no voice. If your throat vibrates, you are making the other TH.

The eight weeks

One sound map.
Then the real work.

The first session is a diagnostic. After that, the program bends around your three target sounds. You record, you practise, you come back. No generic course to sit through.

  1. Week 01

    Sound check

    A 30-minute recorded conversation. You leave with a one-page map: what is clear, what is costing you, and what to practise first.

    30 min live · sound map
  2. Weeks 02–04

    The three sounds

    Minimal pairs, mouth positions, and eight-minute daily reps. Your coach sends a new model every Monday and marks your recordings within a day.

    Daily reps · 3 live sessions
  3. Weeks 05–06

    Rhythm & connection

    The sounds are clean; now they need to survive a sentence. Stress, linking, and the pauses that make you sound certain instead of rushed.

    2 live sessions · phrase drills
  4. Weeks 07–08

    Your real room

    Rehearse the meeting, the interview, the dinner. Final recording, final map, and a practice plan you can keep after the lab ends.

    2 live sessions · final map
What you keep

Your sound map, every model recording, a bank of your own phrases, and a four-week practice plan. No subscription required after week eight.
