How Talk Table works

A table, a host,
and a reason to talk.

Talk Table is not a class with a different name. It is a small, regular conversation with people who are also learning — and one host from Guatemala who keeps it moving.

01

Pick your language and level

Spanish for US learners. English for Guatemalan learners. Tell us where you are and what you want to talk about, and we place you at the right table.

02

Meet the same six people

Your table meets weekly for a season. Same faces, same host, a different question each time. The awkward first ten minutes only happen once.

03

Leave with one sentence

Every table ends with a short voice note: the phrase you want to keep, the correction that landed, and the question you will ask next week.
