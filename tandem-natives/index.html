The week, in two languages.

Best for learners who want to speak more often without homework. You bring what happened this week; your partner brings theirs.

Work · B1–C2

The meeting before the meeting.

For people who use the language at work. Rehearse the update, the pushback, and the follow-up with someone who is doing the same in the other language.

  • 01 Give a two-minute status update with one blocker.
  • 02 Disagree with a decision without softening it into nothing.
  • 03 Repeat back what was agreed so nobody leaves with a different version.

A room with a host

The exchange is
the easy part.

The hard part is finding someone who shows up, speaks at your level, and does not turn the hour into a lesson. That is what the host is for.

  • Vetted partners. Every partner is interviewed, level-checked, and briefed on the format before their first exchange.
  • Prompts before you meet. Three questions land in your inbox the day before, so nobody has to invent a conversation.
  • A host keeps the time. Thirty minutes each way, with a gentle nudge when one language starts taking over.
  • Notes after. The host sends the words you reached for and one thing to try next time.
